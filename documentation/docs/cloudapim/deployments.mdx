# Otoroshi deployments

you can link your [Cloud APIM](https://www.cloud-apim.com) account into your `otoroshictl` config, just do

<Terminal command="otoroshictl cloud-apim login" />

`otoroshictl` shows a code and opens the console in a web browser: check that the console shows the same code, choose
the organisation and what `otoroshictl` may do (read, act on the instances, read their secrets to link them), then
approve. `otoroshictl` gets a personal token of the public API of Cloud APIM, valid 30 days, that you can revoke from
your account in the console. On a machine without a browser, set `OTOROSHICTL_NO_BROWSER=1` and open the page it
prints yourself.

Once logged in, you can list the managed Otoroshi instances of your organisations

<Terminal
    command="otoroshictl cloud-apim list"
    result={`

+------------+--------------+---------+---------+--------+------+--------------------------+-------------------------+
| name       | organisation | version | status  | region | plan | created_at               | id                      |
+------------+--------------+---------+---------+--------+------+--------------------------+-------------------------+
| Production | Acme         | 17.16.0 | running | par    | Prod | 2026-10-08T19:22:26.824Z | mi_vfpcogd0y3egd5ldlxj5 |
+------------+--------------+---------+---------+--------+------+--------------------------+-------------------------+
`} />


and then link one to your `otoroshictl` config (by its name, or by its id when two organisations have an instance of
the same name)

<Terminal command="otoroshictl cloud-apim link Production" />

`otoroshictl cloud-apim logout` revokes the token and forgets it. `CLOUD_APIM_API_URL` points `otoroshictl` to another
console (`https://api.cloud-apim.com` by default).
